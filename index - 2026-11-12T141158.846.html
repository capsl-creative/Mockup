Team & Settings

The primary team can sign up for any shift that isn't restricted to another team. Secondary teams can only sign up for shifts restricted to their team. If someone is on both, the primary team rule applies.

When on, volunteer hours only count once a lead manually marks attendance. When off, hours are credited automatically based on the shift's scheduled time.
